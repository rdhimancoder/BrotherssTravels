Tour Plan

DAY 01: DELHI/AMRITSAR /CHANDIGARH TO MANALI (BY VOLVO)

On Arrival In Delhi catch the Volvo Bus From Delhi Volvo stand ( Majnu Ka Tilla, ISBT, FSK Stadium, RK Ashram Marg Metro Station ) at 5:00 Pm,9:00 ( Chandigarh 11:30 Pm Sec-43 ) and drive for Manali, Overnight Journey in Volvo Bus.

DAY 02: MANALI – LOCAL MANALI SIGHTSEEINGS

Arrive in Manali, check into your hotel, and refresh. Spend the afternoon discovering key attractions including the ancient Hadimba Devi Temple, Vashisht Hot Springs, Tibetan Monastery, and a relaxed evening walk along Mall Road.

DAY 03: MANALI – SOLANG VALLEY / SNOW POINT

Embark on an exciting day trip to Solang Valley. Enjoy breathtaking views of snow-capped peaks and indulge in adventure sports like paragliding, zorbing, and ropeway rides before returning to the hotel.

DAY 04: MANALI – KULLU & MANIKARAN AND KASOL SIGHTSEEINGS

Head out for a scenic excursion across the Parvati Valley. Visit the famous Manikaran Sahib Gurudwara and its natural hot springs, stop by Kasol town, and explore Kullu shawl factories or river rafting spots along the way.

DAY 05: MANALI TO DELHI /AMRITSAR / CHANDIGARH / DEPARTURE

Enjoy a leisurely morning in Manali for last-minute souvenir shopping or café hopping. In the afternoon/evening, board your return AC Volvo bus for an overnight journey back to your origin destination.

DAY 06: MORNING DELHI /CHANDIGARH / AMRITSAR ARRIVAL

Arrive back early in the morning at Delhi, Chandigarh, or Amritsar, marking the end of your memorable mountain trip with sweet memories.
