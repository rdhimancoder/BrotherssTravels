Complete Leh Ladakh Odyssey

Complete Leh Ladakh Odyssey — An ultimate 11-day high-altitude journey starting with pickup from Delhi and covering Leh, Khardung La, Nubra Valley, Pangong Lake, and Tso Moriri.

Pickup & Drop: Delhi Airport / Railway Station
Duration: 10 Nights / 11 Days
Route: Leh → Khardung La → Nubra → Pangong → Tso Moriri
Rating: 5.0 (610 Reviews)

Package Overview

Embark on the ultimate Himalayan expedition through the high-altitude desert of Leh Ladakh. This 11-day comprehensive tour covers majestic mountain passes including Khardung La, the sand dunes and monasteries of Nubra Valley, the stunning color-changing waters of Pangong Lake, and the remote pristine beauty of Tso Moriri Lake, complete with professional mountain pilots and verified accommodations.

Tour Plan

DAY 01: DELHI TO LEH – FLIGHT & ACCLIMATIZATION

Arrive at Delhi Airport/Station and board your connecting flight to Leh (recommended for high-altitude comfort). Upon arrival in Leh, check into your hotel and strictly rest for complete acclimatization to the thin mountain air.

DAY 02: LEH – LOCAL MONASTERY & SIGHTSEEING

After breakfast, embark on a local sightseeing tour around Leh. Visit the historic Leh Palace, Shanti Stupa for panoramic sunset views over the valley, and vibrant Leh Market. Overnight stay at the hotel.

DAY 03: LEH TO SHAM VALLEY SIGHTSEEING

Drive along the Srinagar-Leh highway to explore Sham Valley. Visit the mysterious Magnetic Hill, Pathar Sahib Gurudwara, the confluence of Indus and Zanskar rivers at Nimmu, and Likir Monastery. Return to Leh for the night.

DAY 04: LEH TO NUBRA VALLEY VIA KHARDUNG LA

Drive across Khardung La, one of the highest motorable mountain passes in the world (5,359m). Descend into the lush Nubra Valley and visit the white sand dunes of Hunder for a Bactrian camel safari. Overnight in Nubra camps/hotel.

DAY 05: NUBRA VALLEY TO TURTUK & BACK TO HUNDER

Embark on a scenic day excursion to Turtuk, a historic village near the Pakistan border showcasing unique Balti culture, lush apricot orchards, and stunning gorge views. Return to Hunder for dinner and overnight stay.

DAY 06: NUBRA TO PANGONG LAKE VIA SHYOK ROAD

Drive from Nubra Valley along the rugged Shyok river route directly to the breathtaking Pangong Tso. Witness the mesmerizing color-changing blue waters of the lake. Overnight stay in Swiss camps right beside Pangong Lake.

DAY 07: PANGONG LAKE TO LEH VIA CHANG LA

Enjoy a stunning sunrise view over Pangong Lake. Drive back toward Leh across the formidable Chang La pass (5,360m). Stop at Thiksey Monastery en route. Check into your hotel in Leh for a restful evening.

DAY 08: LEH TO TSO MORIRI LAKE VIA MAHSHE & TSOLTAK

Drive from Leh toward the high-altitude wetland sanctuary of Tso Moriri Lake via Chumthang hot springs. Arrive at Korzok village beside the sparkling blue lake. Overnight stay in camps/guesthouse near Tso Moriri.

DAY 09: TSO MORIRI TO LEH VIA PUGSA VALLEY

Witness a serene morning by Tso Moriri. Begin your scenic return journey to Leh, passing through remote high-altitude plateau landscapes and geothermal springs at Puga. Check into your Leh hotel for dinner.

DAY 10: LEH – LEISURE & SOUVENIR SHOPPING

Spend a relaxed final day exploring Leh Main Bazaar. Shop for authentic Ladakhi pashmina shawls, prayer wheels, apricot jams, and Tibetan handicrafts. Celebrate your successful mountain expedition with a farewell dinner.

DAY 11: LEH TO DELHI – DEPARTURE FLIGHT

Early morning transfer to Leh Airport for your return flight to Delhi Airport/Station, concluding your unforgettable Complete Leh Ladakh Odyssey.
