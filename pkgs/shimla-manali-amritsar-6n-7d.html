Shimla, Manali & Amritsar

Shimla, Manali & Amritsar — A magnificent 7-day cultural and scenic holiday blending the snow-capped hills of Himachal with the spiritual heritage and historic grandeur of Amritsar, starting from Delhi.

Pickup & Drop: Delhi Airport / Railway Station
Duration: 6 Nights / 7 Days
Route: Delhi → Shimla → Manali → Amritsar → Delhi
Rating: 4.9 (380 Reviews)

Package Overview

Embark on a remarkable 7-day journey that combines the pristine pine-covered hills of Shimla and the majestic snow landscapes of Manali with the rich cultural legacy and spiritual aura of Amritsar. Experience peaceful mountain retreats followed by iconic visits to the Golden Temple and the Wagah Border ceremony.

Tour Plan

DAY 01: DELHI TO SHIMLA – ARRIVAL & SCENIC DRIVE

Arrive at Delhi Airport or Railway Station and meet our representative. Enjoy a comfortable private drive towards Shimla through scenic mountain routes. Check into your hotel upon arrival, unwind, and have dinner.

DAY 02: SHIMLA – KUFRI & THE RIDGE

After breakfast, head out for an excursion to Kufri for stunning Himalayan vistas and adventure points. Return to Shimla to explore the historic Ridge, Christ Church, and take a relaxing evening walk down Mall Road.

DAY 03: SHIMLA TO MANALI VIA KULLU VALLEY

Check out after breakfast and drive through breathtaking mountain landscapes to Manali. Pass by Pandoh Dam and Kullu Valley, stopping for local handicrafts or river views. Arrive in Manali, check in, and rest for the night.

DAY 04: MANALI – SOLANG VALLEY & LOCAL SIGHTSEEING

Embark on an exciting day trip to Solang Valley for snow views and adventure activities like paragliding and cable car rides. Later, visit the sacred Hadimba Devi Temple and Vashisht Hot Springs before exploring Mall Road.

DAY 05: MANALI TO AMRITSAR – DRIVE & GOLDEN TEMPLE

Check out after breakfast and embark on a scenic drive from Manali down to the historic city of Amritsar. Upon arrival, check into your hotel and visit the magnificent Golden Temple in the evening to witness its serene night illumination.

DAY 06: AMRITSAR – WAGAH BORDER & LOCAL HERITAGE

Explore Amritsar's rich history with a visit to Jallianwala Bagh in the morning. In the afternoon, take a private drive to the Wagah Border to experience the patriotic Beating Retreat ceremony before returning to Amritsar.

DAY 07: AMRITSAR TO DELHI – DEPARTURE

Enjoy a final breakfast, check out of your hotel, and board your return vehicle for a smooth drive back to Delhi. You will be dropped off at Delhi Airport or Railway Station by evening with cherished memories.
