Spiti Valley Expedition

Spiti Valley Expedition — An exhilarating 10-day extreme high-altitude adventure starting from Delhi and traversing through Shimla, Kaza, Chandratal, and Manali.

Pickup & Drop: Delhi Airport / Railway Station
Duration: 9 Nights / 10 Days
Route: Shimla → Kaza → Chandratal → Manali
Rating: 4.9 (420 Reviews)

Package Overview

Conquer the rugged cold desert of the Himalayas with our Spiti Valley Expedition. This 10-day extreme adventure takes you across towering mountain passes, remote high-altitude villages like Kaza and Kibber, ancient monasteries, and the breathtaking high-altitude moon lake of Chandratal before descending back via Manali.

Tour Plan

DAY 01: DELHI TO SHIMLA – SCENIC FOOTHILL DRIVE

Arrive at Delhi Airport or Railway Station and meet our representative. Embark on a comfortable drive toward Shimla. Check into your hotel by evening, relax, and enjoy dinner.

DAY 02: SHIMLA TO SANGLA / RANGOON VIA KINnaur VALLEY

Drive from Shimla through spectacular gorges of the Sutlej River entering the stunning Kinnaur Valley. Pass Rampur and Jeori to reach Sangla. Check into your camp/hotel and enjoy the evening river views.

DAY 03: SANGLA – CHITKUL (LAST VILLAGE OF INDIA) – NAKO

Visit Chitkul, the last inhabited village near the Indo-China border. Later, drive toward Nako village, famous for its picturesque Nako Lake and ancient monastery perched at 3,662 meters. Overnight stay in Nako.

DAY 04: NAKO TO KAZA VIA TABO MONASTERY & GUE VILLAGE

Explore the 1,000-year-old Tabo Monastery, known as the 'Ajanta of the Himalayas'. Visit the Mummy village of Gue to see the 500-year-old naturally preserved monk mummy before arriving in Kaza. Overnight stay in Kaza.

DAY 05: Kaza – KEY MONASTERY, KIBBER, CHICHAM BRIDGE

Discover the iconic Key Gompa perched high on a hill. Drive up to Kibber village and cross the spectacular Chicham Bridge, one of the highest suspension bridges in Asia. Return to Kaza for the night.

DAY 06: KAZA – HIKMIM, KOMIC & LANGZA (HIGHEST VILLAGES)

Embark on a high-altitude village tour covering Langza (famous for fossil hunting and giant Buddha statue), Hikkim (home to the highest post office in the world), and Komic (one of the highest motorable villages). Overnight in Kaza.

DAY 07: KAZA TO CHANDRATAL LAKE VIA KUNZUM PASS

Drive across the formidable Kunzum Pass (4,551m) down to Batal and onward to the mesmerizing crescent-shaped Chandratal Lake. Witness magical sunset reflections over the crystal blue waters. Overnight stay in Swiss camps near Chandratal.

DAY 08: CHANDRATAL TO MANALI VIA ATAL TUNNEL

Drive from Chandratal through Gramphu and cross the engineering marvel Atal Tunnel to enter lush green Manali. Check into your hotel, unwind, and enjoy a relaxed evening in Manali.

DAY 09: MANALI – SOLANG VALLEY & LEISURE

Spend your day visiting Solang Valley for adventure sports or exploring local cafes in Old Manali and shopping along Mall Road. Dinner and overnight stay at the hotel.

DAY 10: MANALI TO DELHI – DEPARTURE

After breakfast, check out and board your return vehicle for a smooth drive back to Delhi. You will be dropped off at Delhi Airport or Railway Station by evening, concluding your epic Spiti expedition.
