Royal Jaipur & Udaipur Getaway

Royal Jaipur & Udaipur Getaway — An enchanting 5-day royal journey exploring the majestic forts and palaces of Jaipur and the romantic lakes of Udaipur, starting and ending in Delhi.

Pickup & Drop: Delhi Airport / Railway Station
Duration: 4 Nights / 5 Days
Route: Delhi → Jaipur → Udaipur → Delhi
Rating: 4.9 (350 Reviews)

Package Overview

Step into the land of maharajas with our Royal Jaipur & Udaipur Getaway. Explore the iconic pink city of Jaipur with its majestic Amber Fort and City Palace, followed by a romantic escape to the Venice of the East—Udaipur—featuring boat rides on Lake Pichola and breathtaking palace tours, all with verified private cab transfers and comfortable stays.

Basic Details

Date of Travel: Flexible Departures Available Year-Round

Number of nights: 4 Nights / 5 Days

Tour Plan

DAY 01: DELHI TO JAIPUR – PINK CITY ARRIVAL

Arrive at Delhi Airport or Railway Station where our representative will greet you. Embark on a comfortable private drive toward Jaipur. Upon arrival, check into your hotel and spend the evening exploring the vibrant local bazaars of the Pink City. Overnight stay in Jaipur.

DAY 02: JAIPUR SIGHTSEEING – AMBER FORT & HAWA MAHAL

After breakfast, head out to explore the majestic Amber Fort (enjoy elephant ride or jeep ride option), photo stop at Jal Mahal, Hawa Mahal (Palace of Winds), the sprawling City Palace, and Jantar Mantar. Overnight stay at the hotel in Jaipur.

DAY 03: JAIPUR TO UDAIPUR – CITY OF LAKES

Check out after breakfast and enjoy a scenic drive through the Aravalli hills toward Udaipur, the Venice of the East. Check into your hotel upon arrival, unwind, and spend a relaxing evening by the lakeside. Overnight stay in Udaipur.

DAY 04: UDAIPUR SIGHTSEEING – CITY PALACE & LAKE PICHOLA

Explore the grandeur of Udaipur starting with the magnificent City Palace, Jagdish Temple, Saheliyon-ki-Bari, and Fateh Sagar Lake. In the evening, enjoy a romantic boat ride across the sparkling waters of Lake Pichola. Overnight stay in Udaipur.

DAY 05: UDAIPUR TO DELHI – RETURN JOURNEY

After a leisurely breakfast, check out of your hotel and board your private cab for a comfortable drive back to Delhi (or transfer to Udaipur Airport). You will be dropped off at Delhi Airport or Railway Station by evening, concluding your royal Rajasthan tour.
